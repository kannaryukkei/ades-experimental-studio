# BUSINESS-05 — Design Directions

The directions differ in information hierarchy, not merely color and typography.

## Corporate — Meridian Infrastructure

**Direction:** Institutional editorial / proof-led systems narrative.

- First screen: one clear institutional proposition plus a contextual information rail.
- Immediately after the hero: a qualitative three-step proof band (no fabricated metrics).
- Main journey: capabilities → specific capability → contact.
- Page rhythm: strong section dividers, numbered capability rows, lifecycle steps and a dark institutional contact panel.
- Visual language: navy, mineral white and restrained copper; sans-serif typography; structured columns and strong alignment.
- Avoid: generic equal-weight card grids, fake project statistics, unverified certifications and stock imagery.

## Professional services — Fieldnote Advisory

**Direction:** Human, editorial and expertise-led practice.

- First screen: a point-of-view proposition plus a concise "useful starting point" rail.
- Main journey: approach → service → consultation email.
- Page rhythm: statement-led editorial sections, numbered service paths, considered engagement steps and low-friction contact.
- Visual language: warm paper, olive and clay; serif editorial headlines with sans-serif interface labels; purposeful whitespace establishes reading rhythm.
- Avoid: invented consultant portraits, fake testimonials, implied booking systems and inflated expertise claims.

## Product/catalog — Form & Function

**Direction:** Object-led catalogue / fast product discovery.

- First screen: product proposition paired with a clearly identified visual placeholder.
- Main journey: collection → product detail → specifications → inquiry.
- Page rhythm: asymmetric product presentation, product grid, visual detail header, specification table and inquiry CTA.
- Visual language: chalk, graphite and signal orange; geometric sans-serif typography; high-contrast object surfaces.
- Avoid: fabricated photography, unverified material/sustainability claims, checkout/cart affordances and unlabeled placeholders.

## Shared implementation rule

The renderer exposes semantic composition primitives: hero, proof, features, products, story, steps, specs and contact. Each archetype chooses its own content order, variants and stylesheet. A shared primitive does not imply a shared page template. Route, metadata, sitemap, robots, noindex and preview-404 contracts remain unchanged.
